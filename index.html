About Us

A media, marketing and research platform built for Nigeria's real estate sector.

"Access to reliable information is essential for informed investment and responsible property development."

Kano Property Mart (KPM) is a leading real estate media, marketing, and research platform dedicated to promoting transparency, professionalism, and sustainable growth in the real estate sector. Founded by a team of experienced journalists and communication professionals with a strong background in financial journalism and marketing communications, KPM is driven by a passion for ethical, credible, and solutions-oriented reporting.

Through quality journalism, market intelligence, property marketing, research, and stakeholder engagement, we connect investors, developers, professionals, policymakers, and the public with the knowledge and opportunities that drive growth in the real estate industry. Based in Kano — Nigeria's historic commercial hub — KPM showcases investment opportunities, promotes ethical business practices, supports evidence-based policy, and positions Kano as a premier destination for real estate investment.

Our Vision

To be a real estate platform for the emergence of prosperous and secured investment.

Our Mission

To transform the real estate sector through reliable information, innovative marketing, and professional excellence.

Our Story

Founded by a team of experienced journalists and communication professionals with deep roots in financial journalism and marketing communications, KPM was built on a simple belief: that ethical, credible reporting can transform how real estate is invested in, developed, and understood.
